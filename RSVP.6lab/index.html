A deep catalogue, knowledge base and shared-reading platform for the Great Books that form the core of a classical Western education — every entry carries its number in the general list and links to its own book page, where you can open the full card of the work.

How the catalogue is organized

The books are grouped into five historical eras. The running numbering (No. 1 … No. 120) reflects each work's place in the overall canon. One line per book; every link leads to a dedicated page with a description, reading plans and materials for shared discussion — part of the Great Conversation between authors of different ages.
