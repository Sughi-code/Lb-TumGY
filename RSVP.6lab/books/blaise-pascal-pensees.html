Blaise Pascal — Pensées

№ 71 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Fragments for an Apology: greatness and wretchedness of man, the God of Abraham not philosophers, wager under uncertainty, diversion from thinking, hidden Jesus — reason brought to its knees, then comforted.

Historical Context

Jansenist exile from Jesuit Paris, drafted c. 1656–1662 before death at 39; first published 1670 in Port-Royal edit, it became existentialism's and apologetics' shared notebook.
