Aeschylus — Prometheus Bound

№ 4 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

Zeus chains the Titan who stole fire and gave humankind craft, speech and hope; Prometheus refuses submission, naming his crimes as benefits to mortals. The play stages defiant intellect against arbitrary power — the archetypal drama of the rebel for conscience' sake.

Исторический контекст

Attributed to Aeschylus (5th century BC), though scholars long debated its authorship; rediscovered in the Romantic age, it became Shelley's Prometheus Unbound and the patron myth of every art that pits the individual against tyranny.
