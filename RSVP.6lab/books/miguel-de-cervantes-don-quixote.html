Miguel de Cervantes — Don Quixote

№ 63 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

A gentleman reads chivalry romances till he rides out as knight-errant with Sancho Panza: windmills, galley-slaves, the barred library-burning — illusion honored and mocked at once, and the first modern novel laughs at itself.

Historical Context

Part One 1605 after captivity at Lepanto and tax-office disgrace; Part Two 1615 answers a fake sequel by Avellaneda, inventing metafiction four centuries early.
