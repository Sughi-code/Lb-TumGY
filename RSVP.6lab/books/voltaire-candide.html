Voltaire — Candide

№ 77 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

Pangloss insists all is for the best while Lisbon quakes, auto-da-fés burn, Eldorado tempts and gardens get tilled — optimism flogged with fairy-tale velocity.

Исторический контекст

1759, anonymously, days after Seven Years' War scandals and Lisbon earthquake shattered Leibnizian theodicy; Frederick mocked it, Catherine bought copies, and "cultivate our garden" entered the language.
