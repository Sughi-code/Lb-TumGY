Unknown Author — The Song of Roland

№ 44 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Rearguard at Roncevaux: Roland sounds the oliphant too late, Ganelon's treason, die-hard loyalty, and Charlemagne's vengeance — feudal honor sung with blunt, hammering repetitions.

Historical Context

Oldest major French epic, c. 1100, recited by jongleurs before crusading audiences; it codifies vassal-love and holy war, standing beside Beowulf as the charter of medieval heroic poetics.
