Dante Alighieri — The Divine Comedy

№ 47 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

Lost in dark wood at midlife, Dante travels guided by Virgil through nine circles of Hell, up the mountain of Purgatory, and through Paradiso's spheres to Beatrice's smile and the Love that moves the sun and other stars.

Исторический контекст

Composed in Tuscan vernacular c. 1308–1320 during exile from Florence; it fixed Italian, summed medieval cosmology, and its terza rima made poetry itself a vehicle of metaphysics.
