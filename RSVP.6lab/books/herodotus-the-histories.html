Herodotus — The Histories

№ 9 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

A vast inquiry into the Persian Wars that digresses endlessly and brilliantly: Egyptian customs, Scythian geography, Babylon's walls, the debates of Greeks and barbarians. Freedom versus despotism is framed as the cause worth fighting for, told with curiosity rather than condescension.

Historical Context

Composed c. 440–425 BC by an author from Halicarnassus who recited parts at Panhellenic games; Cicero called him "the father of history," Thucydides implicitly rejected his method, and today he stands as the founder of ethnography too.
