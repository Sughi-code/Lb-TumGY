Albert Camus — The Stranger

№ 116 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Meursault indifferent at mother's funeral, sun-struck murder on beach, trial condemning his lack of tears, cosmic indifference embraced before execution — absurd hero's diary.

Historical Context

1942 Gallimard launch alongside Sisyphe essay in occupied France, Algerian settler background; Sartre's "explanation" framed it, and it became absurdism's narrative signature.
