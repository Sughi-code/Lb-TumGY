Aeschylus — The Oresteia

№ 3 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

The only complete Greek trilogy: Agamemnon returns from Troy and is murdered by Clytemnestra; Orestes avenges his father and is hunted by the Furies; at Athens Athena converts the blood-feud into a trial by jury, and the Erinyes become the Kindly Ones. Justice evolves from vengeance to law across three generations of cursed house.

Historical Context

First performed in 458 BC, shortly after reforms transferred the powers of the old aristocratic Areopagus to democratic institutions; the drama sacralizes the Athenian court and marks the moment tragedy speaks directly to civic theory.
