Niccolò Machiavelli — The Prince

№ 51 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Advice to a new prince: arms, fortune, virtù, fox and lion, cruelty well used, and the warning that it is safer to be feared than loved — politics described as it works, not as it should.

Historical Context

Drafted 1513 after Medici restored Florence dismissed and tortured him; circulated in manuscript, printed 1532 and soon blacklisted, it split reason of state from morality and coined "Machiavellian."
