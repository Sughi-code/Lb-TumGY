Isaac Newton — Philosophiæ Naturalis Principia Mathematica

№ 75 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Definitions, axioms, laws of motion and universal gravitation deducing ellipses, tides, comets, precessions — phenomena constrained by geometry, hypotheses non fingent.

Historical Context

Prompted by Halley 1684, printed 1687 by Royal Society; it unified celestial and terrestrial mechanics, crowned the Scientific Revolution, and set the mechanistic worldview Kant would wake.
