Aristophanes — The Clouds

№ 14 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Strepsiades, drowning in debts, sends his son to Socrates' Thinkery, where Argument Worse teaches him to cheat creditors; when the clever pupil beats his father, the old man torches the school. Comic assault on intellectual fashion, sophistry and the corruption of filial duty.

Historical Context

Produced 423 BC, with the historical Socrates caricatured on stage — one root of the later charge that philosophy corrupted Athenian youth; the play remains our earliest witness to Socrates and to Old Comedy's freedom to insult.
