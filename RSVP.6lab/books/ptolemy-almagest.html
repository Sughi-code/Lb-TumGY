Ptolemy — Almagest

№ 40 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

The complete astronomy of antiquity: spheres, deferents and epicycles tabulated with trigonometric tables, star catalogue, solar and lunar theories, predictive apparatus — mathematics doing what philosophy only imagined.

Historical Context

Compiled c. AD 150 in Alexandria; translated into Arabic as al-Majisti, it ruled Islamic observatories and medieval Europe until Copernicus answered it directly — the paradigm Kuhn chose as his example.
