Aristotle — Politics

№ 25 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Man is by nature a political animal; the city exists for the good life, not mere living. Regimes are classified by who rules and for whom, revolution diagnosed and prevented, the middle constitution praised, slavery and education discussed without apology.

Historical Context

Taught while Alexander tutored by Aristotle matured the imperial horizon Athens could not survive; it is the first systematic comparative politics and still structures textbooks on regimes and citizenship.
