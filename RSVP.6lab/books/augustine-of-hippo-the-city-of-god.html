Augustine of Hippo — The City of God

№ 43 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

After Rome's 410 sack, pagans blamed Christianity; Augustine answers with two cities — of earthly love and of heavenly — reading all history as their intertwined pilgrimage toward judgment and the vision of peace.

Historical Context

Composed c. AD 413–426 against Donatist and Pelagian controversies too; it furnished the Middle Ages its philosophy of history, and its just-war and church-state questions remain live.
