Mark Twain — Adventures of Huckleberry Finn

№ 102 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Raft down Mississippi: Pap's violence, duke and king grifters, Jim's humanity, Huck choosing damnation over informing — vernacular voice exposes slavery inside boyish fun.

Historical Context

Written 1876–83 in exile after financial shipwrecks; banned in Concord libraries 1885, rehabilitated by Hemingway's claim all American fiction flows from it.
