William Shakespeare — Hamlet

№ 58 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Ghost, play-within-a-play, delay, "to be or not to be": a scholar-prince tasked with revenge finds action poisoning thought, and Denmark a prison — interiority invented on the public stage.

Historical Context

Written c. 1600–1601 from a lost earlier Hamlet and Saxo's legend; Goethe, Coleridge, Freud and Kozintsev each claimed it, and it remains the West's touchstone of consciousness.
