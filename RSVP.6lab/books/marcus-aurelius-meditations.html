Marcus Aurelius — Meditations

№ 39 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Private notebooks of an emperor on campaign: impermanence, the ruling faculty, duty to the cosmopolis, waking each morning resolved to meet interference and ingratitude without ceasing to be a human being.

Historical Context

Written in Greek c. AD 170–180 in Danubian camps during war and plague; unpublished by design, it survived manuscripts and monasteries to become the stoic bible of William James, George Kennan and countless ordinary readers.
