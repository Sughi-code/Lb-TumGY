Aristotle — Poetics

№ 26 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Tragedy defined as imitation of a serious, complete action arousing pity and fear with catharsis; plot over character, reversal and recognition, hamartia, unity of action; epic and (lost) comedy appended.

Historical Context

Lectures c. 335 BC, recovered in the Renaissance after 1498 Latin printing; it dictated neoclassical unities for two centuries and still supplies screenwriting manuals their vocabulary of arc and turn.
