Jonathan Swift — Gulliver's Travels

№ 76 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Lilliput's pettiness, Brobdingnag's flesh, Laputa's useless science, Houyhnhnms' cold reason and Yahoos' filth — travel satire stripping humanity's pretensions to vermin-size.

Historical Context

Published 1726 under Lambertus Dominicus pseudonym after Whig-exile disappointments; instantly canonical, it founded children's-adventure reception while its misanthropy kept adults unsettled.
