Fyodor Dostoevsky — The Brothers Karamazov

№ 99 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Patricide suspects Dmitri, Ivan, Smerdyakov; Elder Zosima's love-active, Grand Inquisitor poem within — faith, doubt, children's suffering, and Alyosha's stone-by-stone affirmative answer.

Historical Context

1879–80 final novel, months before his death; Freud called it greatest ever, Einstein read it, and its "if God does not exist everything permitted" frames modern ethics.
