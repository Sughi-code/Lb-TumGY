Cicero — On Duties

№ 30 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

A manual for his son: the honestum versus the utile, obligations of justice, beneficence, good faith, the perils of ambition, and casuistry for office-holders. Stoic morality dressed for Roman practical life.

Historical Context

Written 44 BC after Caesar's assassination, as Cicero tried to hand republican ethics to the next generation; printed after Gutenberg as one of the first secular best-sellers, it shaped Erasmus, Enlightenment statesmen and business-ethics syllabi.
