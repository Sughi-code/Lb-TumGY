Friedrich Nietzsche — On the Genealogy of Morals

№ 105 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

Three essays: bad conscience from creditor-debt ritual, ascetic priests breeding nihilism, ressentiment flipping noble values — morality historicized with hammer-proverbs.

Исторический контекст

1887 after Paul Rée quarrel and Fouillée's reviews; it founded genealogical method for Foucault, psychoanalysis's guilt studies, and continental moral skepticism.
