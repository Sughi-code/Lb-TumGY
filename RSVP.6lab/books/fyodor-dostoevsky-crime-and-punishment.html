Fyodor Dostoevsky — Crime and Punishment

№ 98 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Student Raskolnikov murders the pawnbroker to test Napoleon-complex arithmetic, fevers through Petersburg garrets, Sonya's reading of Lazarus, Siberian epilogue of confession and resurrection.

Historical Context

Serialized 1866 after gambling debt and mock-execution trauma; its polyphonic psyche anticipated Nietzsche and Freud's guilty civilization.
