John Milton — Areopagitica

№ 70 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Against the Licensing Order: books not dumb things, truth scattered in pieces, good and evil knowledge inseparable, licensing insults England's maturity — the founding argument for press liberty.

Historical Context

Speech-shaped pamphlet of 1644 amid Presbyterian press controls Milton himself had used; read by Jefferson and Mill as precedent of free-market-of-ideas doctrine.
