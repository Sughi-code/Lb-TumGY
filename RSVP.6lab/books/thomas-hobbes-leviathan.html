Thomas Hobbes — Leviathan

№ 68 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

State of nature as war of all against all; covenant authorizes the sovereign whose sword makes law, religion public, and rebellion irrational — "Mortal God" built from psychology and geometry of the passions.

Historical Context

Published 1651 during Civil War exile under Protectorate; scandalized Anglicans and royalists alike, it remains the charter of social-contract theory and political absolutism's clearest case.
