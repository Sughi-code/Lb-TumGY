René Descartes — Meditations on First Philosophy

№ 67 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Six meditations: senses doubted, dream hypothesis, evil genius, I think therefore I am, wax and imagination, God's existence via perfection, error from will exceeding intellect, matter res extensa.

Historical Context

Published 1641 with Objections from Mersenne's circle (Arnauld, Hobbes, Gassendi); it set epistemology's agenda — foundationalism, mind-body, other minds — still contested.
