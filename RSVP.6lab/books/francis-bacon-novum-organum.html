Francis Bacon — Novum Organum

№ 64 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Idols of tribe, cave, marketplace, theatre cleared away; induction by tables of presence, absence and degrees; heat, weight, form sought — science re-founded on patient experiment, not syllogism.

Historical Context

Published 1620 after Lord Chancellorship's fall for bribery; its methodological manifesto fed the Royal Society and the Enlightenment's cult of useful knowledge.
