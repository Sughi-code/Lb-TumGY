Sigmund Freud — Civilization and Its Discontents

№ 107 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Libido and aggression dammed by superego's guilt; oceanic feeling questioned, happiness priced in security, sublimation, death drive — culture as tragic bargain.

Historical Context

1930 after World War, daughter's death, cancer; addressed to Ferenczi's ghost, it spoke to Marcuse, Fromm and every later psycho-political pessimism.
