Euclid — Elements

№ 29 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

From definitions, postulates and common notions, 13 books build number theory, geometry of magnitudes and proportion by exhaustion — including the prime infinitude, the golden section and the five solids. Proof, not measurement, is the invention.

Historical Context

Systematized earlier results at Alexandria c. 300 BC under the Ptolemies; the most-used textbook ever written, it taught Locke, Newton and Lincoln to reason, and its axiomatic ideal governs mathematics to this day.
