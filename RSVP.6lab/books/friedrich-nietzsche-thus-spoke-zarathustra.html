Friedrich Nietzsche — Thus Spoke Zarathustra

№ 103 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

Prophetic parables: God dead, Übermensch, eternal recurrence, will to power, three metamorphoses camel-lion-child — philosophy performed as scripture against scripture.

Исторический контекст

Parts I–IV 1883–85 after Basel professorship collapse and Lou-Salomé rupture; Wagner-refugee thought turned Wagnerian style against Wagner, influencing Mann, Kafka, Strauss tone-poem.
