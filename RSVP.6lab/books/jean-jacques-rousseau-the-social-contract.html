Jean-Jacques Rousseau — The Social Contract

№ 79 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

Forced to be free: general will indivisible, sovereignty inalienable, legislator godlike, civil religion tolerated — legitimate political association reconstructed from radical equality.

Исторический контекст

Published 1762, burned in Paris and Geneva the same year; Jacobins worshipped it, Tocqueville and Isaiah Berlin diagnosed its totalitarian germ.
