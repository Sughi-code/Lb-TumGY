Dante Alighieri — Vita Nuova (The New Life)

№ 48 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Prose commentary framing lyrics: first sight of Beatrice at nine, her death, visions and consolations — courtly love turned into theology of the beloved as window to God.

Historical Context

Written c. 1292–1294 after Beatrice Portinari's death, among stilnovista experiments; it prepared the Comedy and became the template for Petrarch's Laura and all later autobiographical love-books.
