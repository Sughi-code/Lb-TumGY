Plutarch — Lives of the Noble Greeks and Romans

№ 36 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Paired biographies — Theseus/Romulus, Alexander/Caesar, Demosthenes/Cicero — compare virtue and vice through anecdote and character rather than chronology, asking how great men rose and fell and what readers should imitate.

Historical Context

Written c. AD 100–120 under Trajan and Hadrian by a Delphic priest; Amyot's French and North's English translations made it Shakespeare's Plutarch and the founders' favorite — Jefferson's dinner-table text.
