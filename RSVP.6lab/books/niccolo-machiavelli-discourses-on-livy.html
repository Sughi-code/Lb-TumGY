Niccolò Machiavelli — Discourses on Livy

№ 52 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Reading Rome's first decade: republics outlast princedoms, conflict of nobles and people breeds liberty, religion as civic bond, conspiracies fail, expansion corrupts — mixed constitution defended historically.

Historical Context

Written c. 1513–1519 alongside The Prince for the same exile; adopted by 18th-century commonwealthmen and the American founders as the manual of free-statecraft.
