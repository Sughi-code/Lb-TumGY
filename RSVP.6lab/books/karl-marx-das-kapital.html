Karl Marx — Das Kapital

№ 92 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Commodity fetishism, labor-value, surplus extracted invisibly in consumption of labor-power, primitive accumulation, falling tendency, simple reproduction schemes — capitalism anatomized from within its own categories.

Historical Context

Volume I 1867 from British Museum desks after 1848 exile; Engels edited II–III posthumously; it split economics into schools and powered revolutions claiming its name.
