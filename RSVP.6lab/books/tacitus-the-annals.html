Tacitus — The Annals

№ 38 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

From Tiberius to Nero: principate dissected as school of hypocrisy, informers flourishing, virtue punished, liberty flattered and betrayed — reported in a Latin of telegraphic, poisoned brevity.

Исторический контекст

Composed c. AD 110–120 under Trajan and Hadrian by a consul who had served Domitian's regime; half survives, and its anatomy of unfreedom inside prosperity became the mirror held up to every autocracy since.
