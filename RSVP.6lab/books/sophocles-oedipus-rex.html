Sophocles — Oedipus Rex

№ 5 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

To save Thebes from plague, king Oedipus hunts the murderer of the former king and discovers himself: the child exposed at birth, who solved the Sphinx's riddle and fled his own oracle, has killed his father and married his mother. Sight and blindness, fate and self-knowledge cross in the tightest plot ever written.

Historical Context

Performed c. 429 BC amid the plague and wars of Athens; Aristotle took it in the Poetics as the perfect tragedy — recognition and reversal fused — and Freud later borrowed its name, making it the most analyzed single text in Western culture.
