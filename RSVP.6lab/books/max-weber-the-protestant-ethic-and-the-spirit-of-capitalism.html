Max Weber — The Protestant Ethic and the Spirit of Capitalism

№ 108 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Franklin's maxims as ethos; Luther's calling, Calvinist elect anxiety driving methodical labor, inner-worldly asceticism, iron cage of rationalized modernity — idea-causal sociology launched.

Historical Context

1904–05 essays after American trip and Ritschl controversy; rebutting Sombart and Fels, it became sociology's founding thesis and perpetual battleground.
