Homer — The Odyssey

№ 2 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

Ten years of wandering bring Odysseus home to Iliaca where suitors devour his estate; cunning, disguise, hospitality and reunion with Penelope replace battlefield glory as the poem's virtues. Its nested storytelling, descent to the underworld and faithful dog Argos created the template for every adventure-and-homecoming narrative.

Исторический контекст

Dated to roughly the late 8th century BC, reflecting a world of Phoenician trade and colonial expansion; it became the second half of the Homeric curriculum that every educated Greek and Roman memorized, and its structure echoes through Dante, Joyce and cinema.
