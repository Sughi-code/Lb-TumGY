Virgil — The Aeneid

№ 33 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Troy's refugee Aeneas carries father, gods and household to Italy, loving Dido and losing her, descending to the underworld to meet Anchises and Rome's unborn host, and killing Turnus in anger the poem weighs terribly. Pietas tested by fate and fury.

Historical Context

Written 29–19 BC to Augustus's settlement, deliberately surpassing Homer and Ennius; it became the school-text of empire, the Christian sibyl's prophecy (Fourth Eclogue), and Dante's guide through Hell and Purgatory.
