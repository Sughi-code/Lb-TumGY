Francesco Petrarch — Canzoniere

№ 49 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

366 poems to Laura, alive and dead: sonnets, canzoni, sestinas cycling through hope, regret, self-accusation — passion examined with a humanist's double vision, divided between earth and heaven.

Historical Context

Worked over decades (c. 1330s–1374) by the first "modern" writer, crowned poet on the Capitol in 1415-equivalent ceremony; its Petrarchism dominated European lyric for three centuries.
