Mary Wollstonecraft — A Vindication of the Rights of Woman

№ 85 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Against Rousseau's Émile-woman: education denied produces "spaniel timidity"; reason sexless, virtue same in women, independence sole way to respectable wives and citizens.

Historical Context

1792 amid French Revolution's Declaration row and her own printer-circles; it founded feminist philosophy and stung even admirers like Talleyrand.
