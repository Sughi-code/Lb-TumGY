Francis Bacon — New Atlantis

№ 65 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Christians lost in Bensalem find Solomon's House: organized research, air-ships, oaths of secrecy, and commerce with revelation — techno-utopia with a pious frame.

Historical Context

Left unfinished at Bacon's death 1626, printed 1627; it projected the research institute and inspired colonial and scientific-propaganda utopias.
