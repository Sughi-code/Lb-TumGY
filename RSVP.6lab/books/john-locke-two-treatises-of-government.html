John Locke — Two Treatises of Government

№ 73 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Filmer refuted: paternal power no royal warrant; state of nature sociable, property by labor, trust forfeited by tyranny, dissolution appealing to heaven — limited constitutionalism with a right of resistance.

Historical Context

Secretly drafted for Monmouth rising, published anonymously 1689 after Glorious Revolution; Madison and Jefferson carried its clauses into Declarations.
