Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels — The Communist Manifesto

№ 93 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Bourgeoisie revolutionary of production, proletariat its gravedigger, specter haunting Europe, ten planks, "workers have no country" — pamphlet as compressed world-history of class struggle.

Historical Context

Commissioned by Brussels' League of the Just, published February 1848 days before Paris rising; translated everywhere, it outlived its authors' hopes and empires.
