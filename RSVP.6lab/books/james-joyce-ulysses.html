James Joyce — Ulysses

№ 109 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

16 June 1904 Dublin: Bloom's ad-agency errands, Stephen's tower regrets, Circe hallucination, Penelope's yes — Homeric scaffold under stream-of-consciousness, parody, catechism, every style.

Historical Context

Serialized in Little Review 1918–20, obscenity prosecution halted US print until 1933 Random House; Hughes/Powers translation aid, Woolf's envy, Eliot's endorsement sealed modernism's monument.
