René Descartes — Discourse on the Method

№ 66 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Right use of reason: provisional morals, the cogito glimpsed mid-doubt, God's veracity guaranteeing clear and distinct ideas, mechanics of animals-as-machines — modest prose covering revolutionary demolition.

Historical Context

Published 1637 anonymously with essays on optics, meteorology, geometry; written after the 1633 Galileo affair chilled Descartes, it opens modern philosophy's subject-centered turn.
