John Stuart Mill — On Liberty

№ 94 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Harm principle: society may coerce only self-regarding conduct exempt; tyranny of majority over taste and talk, experiments of living, individuality as utility's ingredient.

Historical Context

1859, year of Darwin's Origin, after Harriet Taylor's influence and Benthamite childhood breakdown; it remains liberalism's working definition of free-speech limits.
