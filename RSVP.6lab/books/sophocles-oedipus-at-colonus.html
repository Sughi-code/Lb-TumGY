Sophocles — Oedipus at Colonus

№ 7 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

The blinded outcast arrives at Colonus near Athens, is taken in by Theseus, and dies not in humiliation but transfigured, promising his burial place as a blessing to the city that shelters him. Suffering is transfigured into sacred power in Sophocles' last play.

Исторический контекст

Written during the final years of the Peloponnesian War and produced posthumously in 401 BC; its praise of Athens and its mysterious theology of the hero's tomb made it both civic testament and enigmatic summing-up of a life spent on fate.
