Jane Austen — Pride and Prejudice

№ 87 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Elizabeth Bennet and Darcy learn self-knowledge through misreading: wit as moral instrument, marriage market dissected without cynicism, letters and balls advancing the plot of judgement.

Historical Context

Revised from First Impressions (1796), published anonymously 1813 amid Regency entail anxieties; it perfected free indirect discourse and the comic novel of manners.
