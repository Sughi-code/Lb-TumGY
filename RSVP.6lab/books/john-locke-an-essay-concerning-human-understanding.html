John Locke — An Essay Concerning Human Understanding

№ 74 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

Mind as white paper; simple ideas from sensation and reflection, complex ones compounded; primary and secondary qualities, personal identity as continuity of consciousness — innateness demolished.

Исторический контекст

Begun with the Cambridge Club 1671, refined in exile, published 1690; it founded empiricism, educated Voltaire, Hume, and developmental psychology.
