Ovid — Metamorphoses

№ 35 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Fifteen books of myth woven into one continuous flow: Daphne into laurel, Phaethon's fall, Arachne's web, Narcissus's pool, human ages hardening from gold to iron, ending with Caesar's star and Ovid's own immortal work.

Historical Context

Completed AD 8, just before Augustus exiled the poet to Tomis for carmen et error; it became the Middle Ages' mythology handbook, source of Gothic sculpture, Titian, Bernini and Shakespeare's imagery.
