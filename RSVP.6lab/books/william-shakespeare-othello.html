William Shakespeare — Othello

№ 61 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

The Moor's marriage to Desdemona poisoned by Iago's stage-managed jealousy: handkerchief, whispers, smothered innocence — racism and self-loathing exploited by motiveless malignity.

Historical Context

Written c. 1603–1604 from Cinthio's tale; Othello's turbaned Venetian-hero status and Iago's soliloquies made it a laboratory of race, gender and manipulation criticism.
