Franz Kafka — The Trial

№ 113 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Josef K arrested without charge, attics of officials, painter Titorelli's three acquittals, cathedral parable Before the Law, throat-cut "like a dog" — bureaucracy as theological horror.

Historical Context

1914–15 fragments published 1925 by Max Brod against testamentary destruction order; Nazi era and Soviet show-trials made it twentieth-century's verdict document.
