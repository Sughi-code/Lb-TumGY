Franz Kafka — The Metamorphosis

№ 114 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Gregor Samsa wakes insect-armored; family feeds him shamefully, sister's violin scene, apple lodged, death swept — alienation of wage-life rendered literally.

Historical Context

Written November 1912 in one sitting after Felice Bauer correspondence; published 1915 Die Weisse Blätter; expressionists claimed it, and it became school-anthology staple worldwide.
