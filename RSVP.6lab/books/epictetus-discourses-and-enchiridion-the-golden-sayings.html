Epictetus — Discourses and Enchiridion

№ 37 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

What is up to us: judgment, impulse, desire; what is not: body, reputation, possessions. Adversity as training, exile as classroom, and the serenity of wanting only what the world can give freely.

Исторический контекст

A freed slave lecturing at Nicopolis after Domitian expelled philosophers (c. AD 95–135); his student Arrian wrote down the Discourses and the handbook; Hadrian admired him, and Hadrian-like Marcus Aurelius proved his method livable.
