Euripides — Hippolytus

№ 13 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Phaedra's nurse betrays her secret love for stepson Hippolytus, devoted to Artemis and virginity; a false accusation destroys the youth, and Poseidon's curse finishes what shame began. The goddess Artemis closes the play with the promise of future rites at Troezen.

Historical Context

Produced 428 BC with prize-winning success; its anatomy of illicit desire, speech and slander made it a touchstone for psychoanalytic readings, and Racine's Phèdre rewrote it for the French classical stage.
