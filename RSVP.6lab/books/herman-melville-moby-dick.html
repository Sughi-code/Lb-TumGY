Herman Melville — Moby-Dick

№ 97 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Ishmael ships out on the Pequod; Ahab's monomaniacal hunt for the white whale drags crew, Queequeg's pact, cetological digressions, and the orphan narrator's survival in coffin-life-buoy.

Historical Context

1851 after Polynesian voyaging and Hawthorne encouragement; initial failure, revived 1920s as American epic — whale as God, machine, obsession, text.
