Martin Heidegger — Being and Time

№ 115 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Question of Being forgotten: Dasein's being-in-the-world, care, thrownness, fallen das Man, anxiety revealing nullity, being-toward-death, temporality as horizon — fundamental ontology.

Historical Context

1927 from Marburg lectures under Husserl's phenomenology, dedicated to his mentor; Sartre, Gadamer, Derrida, Merleau-Ponty all built on or against it, and its 1933 rectorate still shadows reception.
