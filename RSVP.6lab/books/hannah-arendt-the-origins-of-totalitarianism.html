Hannah Arendt — The Origins of Totalitarianism

№ 120 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Antisemitism's secularization, imperialism's raciology and bureau terror, then Nazism/Stalinism as mass movements making men superfluous — totalitarian novelty analyzed comparatively.

Historical Context

1951 after 1943–50 refugee-to-American scholarship years and Eichmann prep; banality-of-evil later developed its premises, and its "right to have rights" anchors human-rights discourse.
