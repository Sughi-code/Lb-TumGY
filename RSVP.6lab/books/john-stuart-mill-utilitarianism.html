John Stuart Mill — Utilitarianism

№ 95 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Qualities of pleasures distinguish higher/lower, rule-of-life hedonic calculus, justice as condensed utility, sanction internal conscience — utilitarianism defended against vulgarization.

Historical Context

Sketches from 1861 Fraser's Magazine essays amid Coleridgean turn; it standardized consequentialism and provoked Sidgwick, Moore, Rawls replies.
