Virginia Woolf — To the Lighthouse

Аннотация

Ramsay house summers and war-gap: Time Passes bracketed decade, lighthouse reached in "Resurrection" section — consciousness, grief, painting's vision, marriage's weather patterns.

Исторический контекст
