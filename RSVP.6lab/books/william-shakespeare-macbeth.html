William Shakespeare — Macbeth

№ 60 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

Victors meet weird sisters, prophecy kindles murder, guilt haunts sleepless kingship — the shortest tragedy is a study of ambition's psychology and Scotland's healing.

Исторический контекст

Composed c. 1606 for James I, flattering demonology and Banquo's royal lineage from Holinshed; its witch-scenes shaped centuries of performance superstition ("the Scottish play").
