Thucydides — History of the Peloponnesian War

№ 10 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

An austere, evidence-driven account of the war between Athens and Sparta, punctuated by set speeches — Pericles' Funeral Oration, the Melian Dialogue's brutal realism, the disaster at Syracuse. Human nature, judged from within the plague-ridden city, is declared "a possession for all time."

Исторический контекст

Written by an Athenian general exiled for losing Amphipolis (424 BC); refusing oracles and marvels, he invented political science and "realist" international relations, quoted by every strategist from Clausewitz to Kissinger.
