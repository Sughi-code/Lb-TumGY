Sophocles — Philoctetes

№ 8 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Nine years marooned on Lemnos with a festering wound and Heracles' bow, Philoctetes is approached by Odysseus and Neoptolemus with orders to steal the arrows needed to take Troy; the young hero's revulsion at deceit becomes the play's moral pivot. Pain, pity and honesty versus military necessity.

Historical Context

Produced 409 BC when Athenian realpolitik abroad clashed with professed civic ideals; Tennyson and many modern dramatists reworked its question — can a good end excuse a rotten means?
