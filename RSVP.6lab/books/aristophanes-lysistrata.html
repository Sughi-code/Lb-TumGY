Aristophanes — Lysistrata

№ 15 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

The women of Greece, led by Athenian Lysistrata, organize a sex strike and seize the Acropolis treasury to force their husbands to end the Peloponnesian War. Bodily farce turns into anti-war satire, with Spartans and Athenians reconciled by appetite.

Historical Context

Staged 411 BC in the crisis year of the Sicilian disaster and oligarchic coup; its pan-Hellenic longing for peace makes it the most performed protest comedy in the modern repertoire.
