Thomas More — Utopian

№ 54 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Travel report from nowhere: elected magistrates, no money, common property, six-hour days, toleration and slaves, religious communities — a mirror showing England its own greed reflected in an island of reason.

Historical Context

Published 1516 in Latin after Amsterdam humanist circles; coined the genre's name, haunted by More's imminent chancellorship and martyrdom under Henry VIII.
