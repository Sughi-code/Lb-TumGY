Georg Wilhelm Friedrich Hegel — The Phenomenology of Spirit

№ 89 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Consciousness ascends through sense-certainty, perception, understanding, self-consciousness, master-slave struggle, unhappy conscience, reason, spirit, religion to absolute knowing — truth as the whole moving.

Historical Context

Finished in Jena under French cannon-shot 1806, published 1807; it seeded Marx's inversion, Kojève's twentieth-century readings, and continental philosophy's historicism.
