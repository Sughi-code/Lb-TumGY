Plato — Symposium

№ 19 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

At Agathon's victory feast, speakers climb from bodily love to love of beautiful souls, laws, knowledge and finally Beauty itself — Diotima's ladder — while drunken Alcibiades bursts in praising Socrates as the living image of that ascent.

Historical Context

Dramatized c. 416 BC, written c. 380s; it fixed the vocabulary of Platonic love, inspired Renaissance commentaries (Ficino), and remains the central philosophical text on eros, friendship and rhetoric.
