Albert Camus — The Myth of Sisyphus

№ 117 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

One truly serious philosophical problem: suicide weighed against absurd divorce of man and world; Don Juan, actor, conqueror, artist as absurd exemplars, Sisyphus imagined happy.

Historical Context

1942 essay after Kierkegaard, Shestov, Jaspers detours and tuberculosis; it paired with Stranger to define existential-humanist option separate from Sartre's later atheism.
