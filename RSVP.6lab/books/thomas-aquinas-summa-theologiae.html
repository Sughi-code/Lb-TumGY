Thomas Aquinas — Summa Theologiae

№ 46 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

The whole of Christian doctrine ordered as questions and articles — God's existence five ways, Trinity, creation, fall, law and grace, Christ and sacraments — answering objections with surgical clarity.

Historical Context

Written 1265–1274 after Aristotle's recovery via Albertus Magnus; left unfinished ("straw") after a mystical halt, it became Catholicism's reference text and the base of neo-Thomist revivals.
