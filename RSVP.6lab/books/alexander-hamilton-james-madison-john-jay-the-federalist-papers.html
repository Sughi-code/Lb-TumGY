Alexander Hamilton, James Madison, John Jay — The Federalist Papers

№ 84 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

Eighty-five newspaper essays urging ratification: extended republic curing faction, energy in executive, senate as check, judiciary as least dangerous branch, bill of rights unnecessary-but-eventually-added.

Исторический контекст

Written winter 1787–88 in New York against Anti-Federalists; still cited by Supreme Court as authoritative original-intent commentary.
