Aristotle — Metaphysics

№ 28 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

First philosophy asks being qua being: substance, actuality and potentiality, form and matter, the categories, and the principle of non-contradiction defended against all comers; theology arrives as thought thinking itself.

Historical Context

Compiled in the 1st c. BC by Andronicus from lectures (4th c. BC) whose editorial order still puzzles; it set the agenda — ousia, essence, existence — for Aquinas, Descartes, Kant and Heidegger alike.
