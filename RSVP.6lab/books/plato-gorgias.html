Plato — Gorgias

№ 22 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

Rhetoric, says Socrates against Gorgias, Polus and the brute-calculating Callicles, is flattery like cookery; better to suffer injustice than commit it, and the unexamined political life is worthless. Callicles' might-makes-right naturalism gets the strongest answer unnamed.

Исторический контекст

Written c. 385 BC when Isocratean rhetoric trained Athens' elites; the dialogue is Plato's declaration of philosophy's war on persuasion-as-power, echoing in every later critique of propaganda.
