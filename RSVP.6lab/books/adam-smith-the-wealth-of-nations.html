Adam Smith — The Wealth of Nations

№ 81 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

Division of labour's pin factory, prices resolved into wages-profit-rent, self-interest feeding invisible supply, mercantilism unpicked, capital accumulation and history of rents — political economy's first architecture.

Исторический контекст

Published 1776 after Toulouse-Bordeaux salon years and American tour; it supplied classical liberalism and Marx alike their vocabulary of value and growth.
