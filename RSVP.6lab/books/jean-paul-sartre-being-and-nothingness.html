Jean-Paul Sartre — Being and Nothingness

№ 118 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Pre-reflective cogito, facticity/transcendence, bad faith waiter and seduction, being-for-others shame of the Look, nothingness at heart of freedom — phenomenological ontology of human reality.

Historical Context

1943 written under Occupation, drawing on Heidegger, Husserl, Beauvoir's corrections; No Exit staged contemporaneously, and it armed French intellectual resistance to determinism.
