Leo Tolstoy — Anna Karenina

№ 101 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

"All happy families alike": Anna's train-platform entrance, Vronsky duel, Levin's mowing and agrarian doubts, Kitty's nursing — adultery tragedy crossed with provincial regeneration.

Historical Context

1875–77 serialization after the Sergius crisis and pedagogical experiments; Dostoevsky hailed it as European-level perfection of the psychological novel.
