Plotinus — The Enneads

№ 41 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Six sets of nine treatises ascending from sensible beauty to Soul, Intellect and the One beyond being; emanation without diminution, evil as privation, and the flight of the alone to the Alone.

Historical Context

Lectures of a Roman teacher (AD 204–270) written down and arranged by Porphyry; baptized by Augustine's reading and by Renaissance Ficino, Neoplatonism saturated Byzantine, medieval and German Idealist mysticism.
