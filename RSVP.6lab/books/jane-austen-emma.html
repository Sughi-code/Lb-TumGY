Jane Austen — Emma

№ 88 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Handsome, rich, self-satisfied Emma match-makes ruinously, misses Frank Churchill's game and Knightley's worth, and is instructed by Mr. Weston's tea — comedy of misunderstanding with a heroine who must grow.

Historical Context

Written during Napoleonic blockade months, published 1815 to the Prince Regent's dedication demand; its "three or four families" canvas hid the deepest analysis of class and self-deceit.
