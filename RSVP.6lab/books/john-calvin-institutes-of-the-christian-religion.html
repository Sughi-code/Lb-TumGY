John Calvin — Institutes of the Christian Religion

№ 56 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Faith organized: knowledge of God and of ourselves, Scripture as lens, providence, election, depravity, church and sacraments, civil authority — systematic Protestant theology in lucid, severe Latin prose.

Historical Context

First edition 1536, expanded till 1559 from Geneva's discipline-state; it shaped Presbyterian, Puritan and Dutch resistance cultures and Atlantic work-ethics debates.
