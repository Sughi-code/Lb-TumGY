David Hume — A Treatise of Human Nature

№ 78 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Ideas copy impressions; causation as custom, self as bundle, reason slave of the passions, ought-is gap, probability belief — empirical science of man pushed to skeptical conclusions.

Historical Context

Written in La Flèche 1734–36, published 1739–40 to "dead-born" silence; it woke Kant from dogmatic slumber and anchors analytic epistemology, ethics and AI debate.
