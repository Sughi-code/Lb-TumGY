Immanuel Kant — Critique of Pure Reason

№ 82 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Copernican turn: objects conform to cognition; space-time pure intuitions, twelve categories, schematism, paralogisms, antinomies, ideal of pure reason — metaphysics bounded, experience saved.

Historical Context

First edition 1781, second 1787 after Hume's alarm and Pietist schooling; it split nineteenth-century philosophy into Neo-Kantian, Idealist and positivist streams.
