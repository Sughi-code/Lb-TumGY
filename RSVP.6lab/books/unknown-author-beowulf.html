Unknown Author — Beowulf

№ 45 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

A Geat comes to Hrotgar's mead-hall, tears Grendel's arm off, kills the monster's mother, and fifty years later dies fighting the dragon — fame measured against the transience of halls and rings.

Historical Context

Unique manuscript c. 1000 in Old English alliterative verse preserving older Scandinavian legend; Tolkien's 1936 lecture made it scholarship, and its elegiac temper informs modern fantasy.
