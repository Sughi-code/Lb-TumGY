Søren Kierkegaard — Fear and Trembling

№ 91 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Silent agonio of faith: Abraham raising Isaac above ethics — teleological suspension, the knight of faith vs infinite resignation, pseudonymous Johannes de silentio trembling at the paradox.

Historical Context

1843 amid broken engagement to Regine Bolette and Hegelian Copenhagen; it opened existentialism and theology's debate on divine command.
