Baruch Spinoza — Ethics

№ 72 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Geometry applied to divinity: one substance, God-or-Nature, infinite attributes, modes following necessarily; affects mapped — joy, sadness, desire — bondage to passion and freedom adequate understanding.

Historical Context

Refused Amsterdam synagogue ban 1656, lens-grinder's life, finished 1675, printed posthumously 1677 and banned; it grounded German Idealism, Deleuze, Einstein's God.
