Geoffrey Chaucer — The Canterbury Tales

№ 50 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Pilgrims from knight to miller tell tales en route to Canterbury: courtly romance, fabliau, sermon, saint's life — genre colliding with class and personality in lively Middle English debate.

Historical Context

Written c. 1387–1400 after Italian sojourns exposed Chaucer to Boccaccio; left fragmentary, it legitimized English as literary language and founded its realist, self-aware tradition.
