Cicero — On the Commonwealth

№ 31 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Dialogue in Scipio's circle on the mixed constitution, justice as the bond of commonwealth, the dream of the just ruler, and the famous closing vision — the Dream of Scipio — of earth's smallness seen from the stars.

Historical Context

Composed c. 54–51 BC in the shadow of collapsing republicanism; largely lost until 1819 palimpsest finds, its Scipionic scheme fed medieval and American ideas of balanced government.
