Sigmund Freud — The Interpretation of Dreams

№ 106 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Wish-fulfilment disguised by censoring work: manifest/latent content, condensation, displacement, infantile wishes, Oedipus sample — unconscious given a deciphering technique.

Historical Context

1899 dated 1900 after Breuer's Studies on Hysteria and self-analysis; initially ignored, adopted by Rank, Jung, Jones, and surrealists, it colonized twentieth-century culture.
