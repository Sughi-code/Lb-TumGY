Aristotle — Physics

№ 27 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Nature as principle of motion and rest: change analyzed through matter and form, privation and the four causes; place, void, infinity, time and chance dissected; the unmoved mover introduced at the summit of the argument.

Historical Context

Core of the Lyceum curriculum (4th c. BC); received wholesale by Islamic and medieval Latin science, it was the framework Galileo and Newton had to break — and its concept of time still provokes philosophers of physics.
