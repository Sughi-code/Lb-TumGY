Plato — Timaeus

№ 23 в общем списке 120 великих книг

Аннотация

Cosmology as "likely story": a demiurge shapes the world from a receptacle after eternal forms, weaving soul through the cosmos with mathematical ratios; the Atlantis digression frames virtuous Athens against hubristic island empire.

Исторический контекст

Composed c. 360 BC; translated partly into Latin by Cicero and Calcidius, it supplied the Middle Ages and Renaissance their chief pagan account of creation, and its geometric atomism prefigured natural philosophy.
