Jean-Jacques Rousseau — Emile, or On Education

№ 80 in the general list of 120 great books

Annotation

Negative tutoring keeps natural goodness unspoiled: senses first, things not books, adolescence's awakening (Profession of Faith of the Savoyard Vicar), Sophie for the companion — education as protection from society.

Historical Context

Same 1762 condemnation as the Contract for its Vicar's deism; it reshaped Pestalozzi, Montessori, Dewey and every child-centered curriculum since.
