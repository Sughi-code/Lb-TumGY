Theme controls

Every color on this site comes from design tokens defined once in css/theme.css (--bg, --surface, --text, --accent…). Pick any color below or switch a preset skin — the change goes through a single central call document.documentElement.style.setProperty('--token', value) and repaints the entire site at once. Your choice is remembered and applies on every page of the site.
